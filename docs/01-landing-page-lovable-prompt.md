# Solar Builder — Landing page update prompt

Update only the landing page of the existing Solar Builder app: https://sun-plan-build.lovable.app/

Use the supplied screenshots as the visual reference. This is a focused content update, not a redesign. Apply the exact replacement copy below.

## 1. Preserve the existing design and navigation

Keep:

- Solar Builder name and logo.
- Existing green, navy and white palette, typography, rounded cards, borders, spacing and overall layout.
- “Demo · synthetic data” badges and the demo’s synthetic data.
- Separate top navigation tabs: My Household, Build a System, Compare, My Checklist and Monitoring. Preserve their routes and behavior.
- Hero heading: “Plan a home solar system, part by part.” Keep the existing green emphasis on “part by part.”
- Both hero buttons, their styling and their existing destinations: “Start with my household” and “See the demo installation”.
- The numbered five-step overview on the right side of the hero. The removal requested below applies to the separate lower “Jump to a step” section.

## 2. Replace the hero subtitle

Remove the paragraph beginning “Like a custom PC builder, but for a Philippine household”. Replace it with:

> Take the power into your own hands. Plan a solar system for your Philippine home, step by step. We help you choose panels, an inverter and batteries for your energy needs and budget, check how the parts fit together, and create a shopping list with estimated costs.

Use the existing subtitle styling and readable line spacing. Keep this as a short paragraph rather than adding more cards or a new section. The message should be clear to someone with little or no technical background.

The copy describes planning assistance and preliminary component checks. Do not add promises of guaranteed compatibility, final installation approval, guaranteed savings or complete energy independence.

## 3. Replace “Jump to a step” with “Our mission”

Remove the lower “Jump to a step” heading and all five shortcut cards underneath it. Replace that entire block with one calm, readable mission section using the existing visual language and page width.

Use the following content in this order.

### Section heading

Our mission

### Subtitle — SDG 7 quotation

> Ensure access to affordable, reliable, sustainable and modern energy for all.

Directly underneath, add this visible, clickable attribution:

[United Nations — Sustainable Development Goal 7](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal7)

### Mission paragraph

> Inspired by the United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goal 7, Solar Builder helps households in the Philippines take the power into their own hands by making solar planning easier to understand. We turn overwhelming technical information into clear steps so people with little or no technical background can learn, compare options and plan around their energy needs and budget. Our wider mission is to support homes and communities across income levels with the knowledge to make their own energy choices and prepare for power disruptions.

### Supporting quote

> “Give a man a fish and you feed him for a day; teach a man to fish and you feed him for a lifetime.”

Present this as a familiar saying in a subtle blockquote. Do not attribute it to the United Nations or an unverified named author.

### Closing sentence

> That is the idea behind Solar Builder: helping people learn how to harness the sunlight around them and take the first steps toward a solar system of their own.

### Mission presentation guidance

- Keep the mission paragraph to the three sentences provided.
- Give the subtitle, attribution, paragraph and quote enough breathing room.
- Use a subtle green accent for the quote if it fits the current design.
- Make the source link accessible and clearly recognizable as a link.
- Frame SDG 7 as inspiration and a goal the platform supports; do not imply UN endorsement, partnership or certification.
- Keep the focus on practical knowledge, confidence and household choice. The wider community mission should not suggest that community system planning already exists in this demo.
- Do not add disaster statistics, claims that disasters affect everyone equally, or claims that solar systems are disaster-proof.

## 4. Remove the landing-page terminology block

Remove the full bottom glossary block containing:

Do not relocate it to another page in this update. Keep this as a follow-up requirement for a separate My Household page prompt: introduce these terms where users begin entering their needs, with short explanations next to relevant inputs.

Implement only this landing-page update. Preserve all other pages, calculations, demo data, saved selections and application behavior.
